I used the **official Bio-Rad second-edition outline** as the topic map, then made these around the likely “2011 gap” topics: CRISPR, nontraditional cloning, digital PCR, isothermal amplification, NGS, DNA barcoding, fluorometric quantitation, and CAR T.
